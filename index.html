    Grinder

    KINGrinder K6 clicks to burr travel to Comandante C40 clicks. Type in any field.

    = =

    One K6 click moves the burrs about 16 µm. One Comandante click moves them about 30 µm, or 15 µm with Red Clix. Both count from zero, the point where the burrs touch, so the same travel gives a similar gap. This is the size of the gap, not the size of the grounds. The burrs are shaped differently, so treat the result as a starting point and adjust by taste.
