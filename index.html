    Grinder

    The same grind on a KINGrinder K6 and a Comandante C40. Type in either field.

    ≈

    How it's built: my own taste notes say K6 80 is like Comandante 25, 100 like 28, and 110 like 30. That's about 6 K6 clicks per Comandante click from 80 upward. Below 80 it runs straight to zero, where both sets of burrs touch, so 3.2 K6 clicks per Comandante click. Published settings agree: Comandante's espresso range (7–13) lands at K6 22–42, and its French press range (26–37) at K6 86–152, in line with K6 settings found online. Red Clix doubles the Comandante number.

    Microns: one K6 click moves the burrs 16 µm, and one Comandante click about 30 µm (15 µm with Red Clix). The burrs are shaped differently, so the same movement doesn't give the same grind. That's why the conversion comes from tasting rather than from microns.
